Abhinav Jha

Assistant Professor · Department of Mathematics · IIT Gandhinagar

I work in numerical analysis and scientific computing. My PhD at Freie Universität Berlin, as a member of the Berlin Mathematical School, focused on stabilization techniques for convection-dominated problems.

As a postdoc at RWTH Aachen and the University of Stuttgart, I moved into computational chemistry, developing domain decomposition methods for Poisson–Boltzmann equations. Brief overviews: AFC schemes, ddLPB, ddPB.
